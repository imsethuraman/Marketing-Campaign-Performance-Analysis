# Marketing-Campaign-Performance-Analysis

This project analyzes the performance of two marketing campaigns (Campaign A vs Campaign B) across multiple channels (Email, Instagram, Web Banner) to identify the most effective strategy for customer acquisition and revenue growth.  The goal is to provide data-driven recommendations to optimize marketing ROI and improve new customer conversion.

🧩 Problem Statement

Marketing teams often struggle to determine:

Which campaign drives higher new customer acquisition
Which channel delivers the best ROI
Where to allocate budget for maximum impact

🛠️ Tools & Skills Used

Excel → Data cleaning & aggregation
Data Analysis → KPI tracking (Revenue, New Customers)
Visualization → Campaign performance comparison
Business Strategy → Recommendation framework

📈 Key Metrics Analyzed

💰 Total Sales
🆕 New Customer Sales
📢 Channel Performance
📊 Campaign Comparison
🔍 Key Insights

1️⃣ Campaign A Dominates New Customer Acquisition

Campaign A generated ~$3,727 in new customer sales
Campaign B generated only ~$2,117
👉 Campaign A is clearly stronger for customer acquisition strategy

2️⃣ Email is the Best Performing Channel

Email contributed the highest sales across both campaigns
Especially strong in Campaign A (~$2,124 new customer sales)
👉 Email is the highest ROI channel

3️⃣ Campaign A Outperforms Across All Channels
Email ✅
Instagram ✅
Web Banner ✅
👉 Consistent performance indicates strong campaign messaging

4️⃣ Campaign B Performs Better in Total Sales (But Not Acquisition)
Campaign B total sales: ~$8,790
Campaign A total sales: ~$5,869

👉 Suggests Campaign B appeals more to existing customers

🎨 Creative Analysis (From Campaign Materials)
Campaign A
Strong CTA: “15% OFF – Don’t Miss Out”
Urgency-driven messaging
Consistent visuals across channels

👉 Drives conversion + acquisition

Campaign B
Premium feel: “Exclusive Offer”
Minimal urgency
More brand-focused

👉 Better for retention, not acquisition

📊 Business Recommendations

✅ 1. Prioritize Campaign A for Growth
Best for acquiring new customers
Strong ROI via Email channel
✅ 2. Scale Email Marketing
Highest-performing channel
Focus on personalization & segmentation
✅ 3. Use Campaign B for Retention
Target existing customers
Position as premium offering
✅ 4. Combine Strategy
Campaign A → Acquisition
Campaign B → Retention

🧠 Strategic Takeaway

📌 The combination of Campaign A + Email channel delivers the highest impact for new customer growth and should be the primary focus for scaling marketing efforts.
